Having been a loyal user of Safe Eyes desktop app on many of my linux machines, I have decided to build something like that for the Windows. I have tried searching for a simple eye break app for Windows but found none that is simple and suit my needs, hence, I have this. The apps out there are either too complicated or they charge, like CareUEyes. This is why I decided to take matters into my hands and build a simple Python based, Tkinter app.

**Core features**

- Remind frequency
- Eye break duration

This is a really simple app that just has these 2 features.
