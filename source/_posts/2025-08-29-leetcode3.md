**Problem:** [Longest Substring Without Repeating Character](https://leetcode.cn/problems/longest-substring-without-repeating-characters/description/)  
**Difficulty:** Medium

---

## 🧠 Approach

This is actually quite straightforward once we can mentally visualise the sliding window. We first have a left bracket at index 0. Then in the for-loop, we iterate to shift the right bracket to find the longest substring without a repeating character. Once we find that the first occurrence of a particular character at right is not equal to right, that means it has occurred somewhere on the left, we simple move the left bracket to 1 past that position.

---

| Solution | _O(n)_ | _O(n)_ |
