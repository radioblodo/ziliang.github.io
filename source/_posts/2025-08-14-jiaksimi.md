“**Jiak simi?**” (what to eat?) — I built **Jiaksimi.ai** to answer that in seconds.  
Pick the ingredients you already have and get **5 great YouTube recipes**. Not cooking? I also show **5 nearby restaurants** using your location. Try it out **[here](https://radioblodo.github.io/jiaksimi/)** 

---

## 🍳 What it does

- Ingredient picker → **generates 5 relevant YouTube recipe videos**
- Location-aware suggestions → **5 restaurants nearby** with links to open in Google Maps
- One-click copy/share of a chosen recipe or place
- Mobile-first UI for quick decisions at the grocery, in the kitchen, or out and about

---

## 🛠️ How it works (high level)

1. **Ingredient selection**  
   Users choose ingredients (e.g., *chicken*, *garlic*, *rice*). Jiaksimi builds a search phrase and fetches a small, diverse set of recipe videos from YouTube, then surfaces the **top 5**.

2. **Video suggestions**  
   Results are ranked for clarity and relevance (title/description match, recency, channel quality), and de-duplicated so you don’t get five versions of the same dish.

3. **Restaurants nearby**  
   With the browser’s **Geolocation API**, the app requests your location and uses **Google Maps / Places** to find close restaurants. It shows **5 spots** with rating, distance, and a deep link to open right in Google Maps.

4. **Fast & simple UX**  
   Everything happens on one page: swap ingredients, refresh videos, or tap the **Eat Out** tab to see places around you.

## 🧰 Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Vue.js  
- **APIs:** YouTube search (for recipes), Google Maps / Places (for nearby restaurants)  
- **Other:** Browser Geolocation, plain CSS/utility classes

## ✨ What I learned

- Designing a **decision-making UI** that reduces choice overload
- Handling **geolocation permissions** and fallbacks when users deny location
- Normalizing video search results and avoiding duplicates
- Small performance wins with result caching and cheap re-ranking
